# SignalWord Build 8 — App Review demonstration script

Read the quoted narration aloud, or use it as captions. The **Show** lines tell you what to record. Pause on each result so Apple can read it. Multiple clips can be combined in this order; keep the action and its actual result together. This is a functional review demonstration, not a short promotional video.

Before starting, follow the [recording preparation checklist](RECORDING_GUIDE.md). Use a disposable demonstration account and a consenting recipient inbox you control. Keep `review@signalword.app` for the final login scene. Finish any existing alert or timer before switching accounts. Do not display passwords, device passcodes or unrelated inbox messages.

Replace the device/OS brackets below with your real information. Use only narration that matches the results actually shown.

## 1. Launch and identify the build

**Show:** Begin on the iPhone Home screen and open SignalWord. Identify the device/OS with a caption. You can briefly show TestFlight's installed **1.0 (8)** version after this launch, or include that shot as an appendix; return to SignalWord to continue.

> “This is SignalWord version 1.0, Build 8, running on a physical [iPhone model] with [iOS version]. SignalWord helps users keep a consenting trusted person informed through private alerts and safety check-ins. It does not contact emergency services or guarantee help.”

## 2. Create a demonstration account

**Show:** From onboarding choose **New to SignalWord? Create account**. Enter the disposable email, tap **Verify and request code**, complete CAPTCHA, enter the emailed code and tap **Sign in**. Show the successful transition. Trim email waiting time if needed, without implying a skipped verification step.

> “I am creating a separate demonstration account. Registration uses CAPTCHA and an email verification code. This confirms access to the email address. I will delete this demonstration account later and keep the reviewer account available.”

## 3. Set up and confirm a trusted contact

**Show:** Complete the introduction. Enter demonstration names and the consenting recipient address. Tap **Send confirmation**. Open the matching recipient email and confirm consent in its browser page. Return to SignalWord and refresh the contact status until it shows **Email confirmed**.

> “A trusted person must agree before receiving alerts. I send a confirmation request, and the recipient confirms it through their email and browser. The recipient does not need another SignalWord account or a Google account. The app now shows that their email is confirmed.”

## 4. Send a TEST and acknowledge it

**Show:** With no active alert, tap **Send TEST alert** once. Show the matching TEST email and its private recipient page. Acknowledge it. Return to the app and tap **Refresh status**; show the actual delivery and acknowledgement information.

> “This is a clearly labelled TEST alert. The recipient opens the private link and acknowledges it. Back in SignalWord, I refresh the status to see the response. Delivery and acknowledgement are separate. Acknowledgement does not resolve the alert or mean that help is coming.”

**Also show:** The normal REAL-alert entry point or its explanation, without activating it for this rehearsal.

> “The normal alert route is separate from this TEST rehearsal. For this demonstration I am using TEST so the recipient knows it is practice.”

## 5. Resolve the alert

**Show:** Use **Hold to resolve this alert** or its **Review and confirm** alternative. Complete device authentication if requested. Show resolved status and the matching resolution email.

> “I explicitly resolve the alert. SignalWord now shows it as resolved and sends the recipient an informational resolution email. The recipient does not need to confirm that email.”

## 6. Complete a safety check-in

**Show:** Home > **Safety check-in** > **Start 15-minute check-in**. Wait for **Active — confirmed by server** and the deadline. Tap **Check in now**, then **Refresh timer status**. Show **Checked in — confirmed by server**.

> “A safety check-in is managed by the server. I wait for the server to confirm the timer, then check in before the deadline. The server now confirms my check-in. Closing the app does not cancel a timer. A missed check-in can create a REAL alert after the grace period and scheduling run.”

## 7. Demonstrate the optional voice route

**Show:** In Apple Shortcuts, show a saved shortcut using SignalWord's **Send TEST Alert** action. Show the practice phrase configured in iPhone Settings > Accessibility > **Vocal Shortcuts**, then return to the app and demonstrate it. Show the resulting TEST and resolve it before continuing. Record only a phrase you are comfortable sharing with Apple.

> “SignalWord integrates with Apple Shortcuts. For rehearsal, I configured a practice phrase to run Send TEST Alert through Apple's Vocal Shortcuts. iOS handles recognition; SignalWord does not continuously listen or collect the phrase. Trigger Alert is the separate REAL action.”

Only say the next sentence if the action actually succeeds on screen:

> “The practice phrase has created this TEST alert. I will resolve it before continuing.”

If you show configuration only, say that clearly; do not describe it as a completed voice test.

## 8. Explain optional location

**Show:** Settings' optional location information and permission choice. If showing a location snapshot, use a controlled TEST, show its availability/freshness information, and resolve the alert. Keep personal coordinates out of shared copies.

> “Location is optional. When available and permitted, SignalWord can include a location snapshot. This is not continuous tracking, and denying location permission does not prevent alerts.”

## 9. Show paid appearance, persistence and restore

**Show:** Settings > **Supporter appearance**. Select Ocean, return Home, close and reopen the app. Repeat with Lavender. Show both remain selected after reopening. Tap **Restore purchases**, then **Refresh purchase status**, and show the actual results.

> “SignalWord is free to download, and all safety features are free. One optional, non-consumable Supporter purchase unlocks both Ocean and Lavender accents and a supporter card. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, and does not change alert priority or delivery.”

For your already-owned purchase, say:

> “My store account already owns Supporter, so this screen shows the unlocked themes instead of another purchase button. I can switch between both themes, and the selection remains after reopening. Restore purchases is available on this same screen.”

For an unowned store account instead: show **Unlock appearance** with its actual localized price and Apple's purchase sheet; complete it only if intentionally testing the purchase. Do not claim to demonstrate a new transaction when you demonstrate existing ownership/restoration.

## 10. Show consent controls

**Show:** On the disposable account, show People > **Withdraw this contact** and the recipient confirmation page's **Withdraw trusted-contact consent** before withdrawing. Complete one withdrawal after all alert/check-in tests. To demonstrate both completed operations, establish fresh consent between them.

> “Both the sender and the recipient can withdraw contact consent. Withdrawal stops future and unclaimed sends; an email already submitted to the delivery provider cannot be recalled. SignalWord has no public feed or open chat. Abuse concerns can be sent to the support address.”

## 11. Delete only the demonstration account

**Show:** Confirm no alert or timer remains active. Settings > **Delete account and data**. Show the confirmation, complete it and wait for the successful return to onboarding.

> “Account deletion is available inside the app. I am deleting only the disposable demonstration account. This is different from signing out or uninstalling the app.”

After actual success:

> “The deletion completed, and the app has returned to onboarding.”

## 12. Finish with the working reviewer login

**Show:** Choose the existing-account route > **Use an existing password**. Enter `review@signalword.app` and the current password privately. Tap **Verify and sign in** and complete CAPTCHA. Show Home and the confirmed contact in People. Close and reopen once to demonstrate the restored session. If showing Sign out explicitly, do so with no active alert/timer and repeat password login; do not delete this account.

> “I am now using the dedicated reviewer account supplied in App Store Connect's private sign-in fields. Password login does not require access to my email inbox. The saved contact is present, and the session is restored after reopening. Reviewers can configure a recipient inbox they control to independently test receipt and acknowledgement.”
