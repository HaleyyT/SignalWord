SignalWord 1.0 (8). The physical-iPhone recording and expanded answers are supplied in this submission's App Review conversation.

PURPOSE
An iPhone utility for private, consenting-contact alerts and server-managed check-ins. No medical care, emergency dispatch, rescue service or delivery/help guarantee. Internet and working delivery services are required.

ACCESS
Use review@signalword.app and the password in the private sign-in fields. Existing-account route > Use an existing password > Verify and sign in; complete CAPTCHA. No Google login or access to our inbox is required. Create account uses CAPTCHA and an emailed code. Settings has Sign out and Delete account and data; use a disposable account for deletion.

CONTACT AND TEST
People shows the confirmed contact. To inspect receipt, add/replace a contact with an inbox you control and confirm its consent email in the browser. Recipients need email/browser, not an app/account. Our existing recipient inbox is not accessible to you. New invitations reset consent and TEST evidence.

With no active alert, tap Send TEST alert once. In the recipient email, acknowledge the private TEST link. Return > Refresh status. Acknowledgement is separate from resolution and does not mean help is coming. Hold to resolve this alert, or use Review and confirm. The resolution email needs no confirmation.

Home > Safety check-in > Start 15-minute check-in. Wait for Active — confirmed by server, then Check in now > Refresh timer status. Verify Checked in — confirmed by server. Missing a deadline may create a REAL alert after grace/scheduling, even with the app closed; ending a timer does not retract an existing alert.

OPTIONAL VOICE/LOCATION
Save SignalWord's Send TEST Alert action in Apple Shortcuts. Settings > Accessibility > Vocal Shortcuts assigns a practice phrase. Trigger Alert is REAL; do not use it for rehearsal. iOS handles recognition; SignalWord does not continuously listen/collect the phrase. Locked/background operation is best effort. Location is an optional snapshot; denial does not prevent alerts.

PRIVATE CONTENT
No public feed, open chat or public content catalogue. People > Withdraw this contact and the recipient confirmation page's Withdraw trusted-contact consent stop future/unclaimed sends. Already submitted emails cannot be recalled. Abuse concerns: support@signalword.app; no in-app report feature is claimed.

SERVICES
Supabase: auth/database/functions. Resend: transactional email. Cloudflare: CAPTCHA, domain/DNS and mailbox routing. Vercel: public/recipient/auth web pages and API routing. RevenueCat + Apple StoreKit: purchase entitlement/restoration. Apple frameworks: Shortcuts/Vocal Shortcuts, location, secure storage, device authentication and reminders. Recipient Open in a map opens external OpenStreetMap; no embedded map tiles/media catalogue. No generative AI/danger-detection service. Sentry collection is disabled in Build 8.

REGIONS/REGULATED SERVICES
No app-defined regional core-feature variants; English interface. Store price/currency/tax/availability and Apple voice-feature availability can vary. No medical diagnosis/treatment, financial, gambling, professional rescue or emergency-dispatch service.

PURCHASE
Free app and safety functions. Settings > Supporter appearance > Unlock appearance (localized Apple price). com.signalword.supporter.appearance is one non-consumable purchase unlocking BOTH Ocean/Lavender accents and a supporter card, not a subscription. Already-owned purchases show choices instead of a buy button. Restore purchases and Refresh purchase status are here. No safety priority/delivery benefit or reviewer bypass.
