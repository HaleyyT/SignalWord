# Physical-iPhone recording for Apple's information request

Record the actual installed TestFlight **SignalWord 1.0 (8)** on a physical iPhone. Use the latest publicly released iOS version supported by that device, as Apple requested. A simulator, marketing animation or a different local build is not this evidence. This App Review recording can be longer than the separate Shipaton demo; cover the requested flows clearly rather than cutting them to a hackathon time limit.

## Before recording

1. Check TestFlight's installed version, and note the actual iPhone model/iOS version from Settings > General > About. Give those facts to Apple with the recording; do not guess them or claim an OS you did not use.
2. Keep `review@signalword.app` enabled with its working password and confirmed contact. Verify that Apple's private sign-in fields contain the current working credentials. Do not put a password in this repository, video caption or public description.
3. Choose a separate disposable demonstration email/account that you control. It needs email-code access for registration. Do not use the reviewer account or your personal account for the deletion demonstration.
4. Arrange a consenting recipient inbox you control. Recipients need email and a browser, not another app or Google account. Have the matching confirmation, TEST and resolution messages available in sequence.
5. Resolve any active alerts and end/check in any active timer before signing out. Use Settings > Sign out to reach onboarding before recording; never delete an account just to switch users.
6. Turn on Do Not Disturb to avoid unrelated notifications. Keep personal inbox contents, passwords, one-time codes and private recipient-link tokens out of public copies. Supply the review footage privately to Apple. Keep the actual app results readable; do not replace errors with an edited success screen.

## Recording sequence

Start recording on the iPhone Home screen, then launch SignalWord. An introductory caption can identify the device, actual iOS version and Build 8. Record enough of each server-confirmed result for a reviewer to read it.

| Chapter | What to show | What Apple should see |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Launch and registration | Choose **New to SignalWord? Create account**, enter the disposable email, tap **Verify and request code**, complete CAPTCHA, enter the emailed code and tap **Sign in**. | New-account creation and email confirmation occur in the ordinary protected flow. This route does not set a password automatically. |
| Introduction and contact | Complete the introduction; enter synthetic display names and the controlled recipient email. Send confirmation. Open that matching email's consent page and confirm; return to SignalWord and refresh confirmation. | The app explains its purpose and the recipient explicitly agrees. Status becomes **Email confirmed**. |
| TEST and recipient response | Tap **Send TEST alert** once. Open its matching TEST email and recipient link, acknowledge it, return to SignalWord and **Refresh status**. | A TEST-labelled rehearsal with separate delivery and acknowledgement information. No REAL alert is needed. |
| Resolution | Use **Hold to resolve this alert**, or **Review and confirm**, and complete device-owner authentication if requested. Show resolved status and the matching informational resolution email. | Acknowledgement and explicit resolution are different states. |
| Safety check-in | Home > **Safety check-in** > **Start 15-minute check-in**. Wait for **Active — confirmed by server**. Tap **Check in now**, refresh and show **Checked in — confirmed by server**. | A real server-backed timer completed before its deadline. Do not wait for escalation or leave an active timer behind. |
| Voice route | Show SignalWord's TEST action saved in Apple Shortcuts. If configured, demonstrate the TEST practice phrase through Vocal Shortcuts, then acknowledge/resolve that additional TEST too. | The iOS integration uses the TEST action. **Trigger Alert** is the REAL action and is not a rehearsal. If setup or permissions fail, report it; do not claim a voice success that was not recorded. |
| Optional location | Show Settings' optional location explanation and the choice to allow location. If you demonstrate a fresh snapshot, use a controlled TEST and resolve it afterwards. | Location is optional and labelled with availability/freshness, not continuous tracking. Avoid exposing a home address or exact personal coordinates in shared footage. |
| Paid appearance | Settings > **Supporter appearance**. If unowned, show the localized **Unlock appearance** button and Apple's purchase sheet. If already owned, show the supporter card and both unlocked choices. Select Ocean, show Home, relaunch; repeat Lavender. Tap **Restore purchases** and show its actual result. | One purchase unlocks both themes. Already-owned purchases can legitimately hide the buy button. Restore is available; all safety features remain free. |
| Consent controls | Show **Withdraw this contact** in People and **Withdraw trusted-contact consent** on the recipient confirmation web page before withdrawing. Complete one withdrawal after all TEST/timer demonstrations. If demonstrating both complete operations, establish fresh consent between them. | Both sides have consent controls. There is no public feed/chat or invented in-app report/block feature. Support handles abuse concerns. |
| Account deletion | With the disposable account still signed in and no active alert/timer, Settings > **Delete account and data**, then confirm the destructive action. Wait for the actual success/onboarding result. | In-app deletion, not merely uninstalling or signing out. Do not delete the dedicated reviewer account. |
| Reviewer password login | From onboarding, select the existing-account route > **Use an existing password**. Enter `review@signalword.app` and its current password privately, tap **Verify and sign in**, complete CAPTCHA. Show Home/People and its existing confirmed contact; close/reopen once. | The reviewer can enter without your inbox, the contact is retained and the session restores. Finish with this account available for Apple. |

If your store account already owns Supporter, do not repeatedly purchase it, delete the store account, or remove the reviewer account to force a purchase button. Show access to paid features and successful restoration honestly. State in the response that the demonstration uses an already-entitled store account; reviewers without the entitlement see the purchase button.

If any requested operation fails, retain the result and report the exact operation/build/time before resubmission. Do not describe the video as covering a chapter that it omits. If you split the footage into chapters, every chapter must be actual physical-device footage with a clear order.

## Hand off the footage

Play the whole recording once: confirm the first scene launches SignalWord, the version is Build 8, text is readable, account creation/deletion/login and paid access are included, and all TEST alerts/timers are left resolved/completed. Give Apple the actual device/OS facts. Attach the recording in the private App Review reply if the interface accepts it; otherwise use a reviewer-accessible video link and verify it opens without requesting your login or an access approval.
